Blowfish

Block cipher with symmetric secret key

Published 9 March 2020

  • Block length = 64 bits
  • Key length: from 32 bits up to 448 bits

Designed in 1993 by Bruce Schneier, an American cryptographer.

Contents

Usage

It is used in plenty of encryption products and provides a good encryption quality. There is no known effective cryptanalysis of this cipher.
