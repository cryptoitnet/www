Online cipher tools

Encrypt and decrypt classical ciphers or convert text to UTF-8. Each tool sits next to an explanation of the algorithm. Everything runs in your browser – the text you enter is not sent anywhere.

  • Playfair cipher online

    Digraph substitution with a 5×5 table built from a keyword.

  • Two-square cipher online

    Two key tables, horizontal or vertical layout.

  • Four-square cipher online

    Four 5×5 tables, two of them built from keywords.

  • Rail fence cipher online

    Zigzag transposition with a chosen number of rails and offset.

  • Route cipher online

    Text written into a grid and read along a spiral.

  • UTF-8 converter online

    Text ↔ UTF-8 bytes as hex, binary, decimal or HTML entities.
